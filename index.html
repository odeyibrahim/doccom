Butterfly Effect Dashboard

Transforming event management from chaotic coordination to elegant orchestration

The Challenge

Event organizers were managing fashion pop-ups through a fragmented collection of spreadsheets, emails, and messaging apps. This disjointed approach created communication gaps, missed details, and administrative overwhelm that distracted from the creative aspects of event production.

Our Approach

We immersed ourselves in the world of event production, shadowing organizers through multiple event cycles. Through this deep understanding, we designed a centralized dashboard that brings clarity to complexity—transforming chaotic coordination into an orchestrated workflow where every detail finds its place.

The Results

Within 3 months of implementation, event organizers reported a 60% reduction in administrative time, 45% improvement in vendor communication, and 30% increase in event profitability through better financial tracking. The platform has become an essential tool for managing multiple concurrent events.

Key Features

  • Vendor management with status tracking
  • Inventory tracking with real-time updates
  • Financial dashboard with expense tracking
  • Task management with team assignments
  • Marketing campaign planning and execution

Coal By Jacklyn

Where fashion e-commerce meets artistic expression

The Challenge

Coal By Jacklyn needed an online presence that reflected their brand's artistic sensibility while providing a seamless shopping experience. Their previous website failed to capture the brand's aesthetic and made the shopping process feel transactional rather than experiential.

Our Approach

We approached this project as a collaboration between commerce and art. The design prioritizes visual storytelling through immersive lookbooks while maintaining intuitive navigation and frictionless purchasing. Every interaction was crafted to feel like browsing through a carefully curated collection rather than shopping on a typical e-commerce site.

The Results

The new platform increased average session duration by 3.2x and reduced bounce rate by 68%. Most importantly, it captured the brand's essence so effectively that it became a marketing asset in itself, with customers frequently mentioning the website experience in social media posts about the brand.

Key Features

  • Immersive lookbook experience
  • Seamless product discovery and filtering
  • Integrated size guide and styling recommendations
  • Streamlined checkout process
  • Content management for seasonal collections

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with Ray of Light for your digital projects. Can't find what you're looking for? Contact us directly for personalized answers.

Getting Started

How do I get started with Ray of Light?
Getting started is easy! Simply reach out through our contact form or use the chat feature. We'll schedule a complimentary consultation to discuss your project needs, goals, and technical requirements. Based on our conversation, we'll provide a tailored proposal that outlines the recommended approach, timeline, and investment.
What information should I prepare for our first meeting?
For our initial consultation, it's helpful if you can share your business goals, target audience, technical requirements, and any existing systems or platforms. Don't worry if you don't have all this information—we're here to help you clarify your vision and technical strategy.
How do you determine which approach is right for my project?
We take a strategic approach to understand your business objectives and technical requirements first. Based on your goals, user needs, and technical constraints, we'll recommend the approach that will deliver the greatest impact. We consider factors like scalability, performance, and integration requirements.
Do you offer free consultations?
Yes, we offer a complimentary 30-minute consultation to discuss your project needs and determine if we're a good fit for your business. This is an opportunity for us to understand your goals and for you to learn more about our process.

Our Process & Approach

What's your typical process for a web application project?
We follow a structured 4-phase process: 1) Discovery & Strategy where we deeply understand your business, users, and technical requirements; 2) Design & Prototyping where we create user flows and interactive prototypes; 3) Development & Implementation where we build and test the application; and 4) Launch & Optimization where we deploy and monitor performance. Each phase includes collaboration and feedback sessions.
How does your approach differ for dashboard projects vs. consumer websites?
Dashboard projects focus on data visualization, complex interactions, and user efficiency, while consumer websites prioritize conversion, storytelling, and brand experience. Our approach adapts to each project type, ensuring the right balance of functionality, usability, and visual appeal for the specific context and users.
We already have an existing system. Can you work with what we have?
Absolutely! We frequently work with existing systems and platforms. We can design new interfaces for legacy systems, integrate with existing APIs, or rebuild specific components while maintaining compatibility with your current infrastructure. We'll conduct a technical assessment to understand your existing environment and recommend the best approach.
How many revisions do you include in your projects?
We typically include 2-3 rounds of revisions for each major deliverable. This allows for refinement while keeping the project on track. Additional revisions can be accommodated if needed, and we'll discuss any potential impact on timeline and budget beforehand.

Execution & Delivery

How long does a typical web application project take?
Most web application projects take 8-16 weeks, depending on complexity and specific requirements. Dashboard projects typically take 6-12 weeks. We'll provide a detailed timeline after our initial consultation based on your specific needs and the scope of work.
What if we need help after our project is complete?
We offer ongoing support and maintenance packages to help you optimize performance, add new features, and ensure your application continues to meet your business needs. Many clients find value in having a dedicated technical partner for their evolving requirements.
What technical standards do you follow?
We follow industry best practices for web standards, accessibility (WCAG), performance, and security. Our code is clean, documented, and follows modern development patterns. We ensure cross-browser compatibility and responsive design for all devices.
Do you provide training on how to use our new application?
Yes, we include comprehensive training and documentation as part of our web application packages. We'll walk your team through the system, explain key features, and provide administrator training. For dashboard projects, we include specific training on data interpretation and system management.

Investment & Value

What makes your development process different?
Our approach is user-centered and business-focused from the start. We begin by understanding your business objectives and user needs, ensuring that every technical decision supports your goals rather than just following trends. We focus on creating "useful technology" that not only functions beautifully but also drives measurable business results.
Do you offer payment plans?
Yes, we offer flexible payment options for larger projects, typically with a 50% deposit to begin work and the remainder upon completion. Support services are billed monthly. We believe in transparent pricing with no hidden fees, and we'll clearly outline all costs in your proposal.
How do you measure the success of our digital products?
We establish key performance indicators (KPIs) at the beginning of each project based on your business goals. These might include metrics like user engagement, conversion rates, task completion times, system performance, and business process efficiency. We believe technology should deliver measurable ROI, not just functional improvement.
What's included in your retainer services?
Our retainer services provide ongoing technical support that can include: performance monitoring, feature enhancements, security updates, technical optimization, and user support. We tailor each retainer to the specific needs of the client, ensuring you get the most value from our partnership.
Do you offer discounts for startups or non-profits?
Yes, we offer special pricing for early-stage startups with limited budgets and non-profit organizations. We believe in supporting organizations that are making a positive impact. Contact us with details about your organization, and we'll create a customized proposal that fits your budget.
Still have questions?

We're here to help. Send us a message through the chat or contact us directly for personalized answers to your specific questions. No question is too technical!
